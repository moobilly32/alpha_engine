  HOW TO READ THIS
  The DiD row at the PRIMARY horizon is the answer. Positive and
  significant = the score carries information. Straddling zero = it does
  not, on this evidence.
  Four horizons are tested, so a Bonferroni-corrected threshold is
  p < 0.0125. Secondary horizons clearing 0.05 but not 0.0125 are not
  findings.
  A null here is WEAK evidence of no effect, not proof of none: cohorts
  of this size over 59 sessions can only detect a fairly large effect.
